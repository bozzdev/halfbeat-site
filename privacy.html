1. What information we collect

In short: We collect information you give us, information generated automatically when you call, and call recordings.

Information you provide

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you telephone a business that uses our service, register on our services, or contact us. This may include:

  • Names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Mailing and service addresses
  • Usernames and passwords
  • Contact preferences
  • Billing addresses
  • Voice recordings and call transcripts
  • Service request details, including the nature and urgency of the problem reported

Sensitive information. We do not process sensitive personal information.

Payment data. We use Stripe to process payments from our business clients. Payment information is transmitted directly to Stripe and is not stored on our systems. Stripe's handling of that information is governed by their privacy policy. We do not collect payment information from callers.

Information collected automatically

Some information is generated automatically rather than provided by you:

  • Call data. Your phone number as delivered by caller ID, the date, time, and duration of the call, an audio recording of the call, an automatically generated transcript, and technical identifiers associated with the call.
  • Log and usage data. Service-related, diagnostic, and performance information our systems record, such as timestamps, system activity, and error reports.

Google APIs. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information from other sources

We receive information about you from our telephony and voice service providers, which deliver your phone number, call metadata, and call audio to us. We may also receive customer or membership information from our business clients so that the assistant can recognize returning callers.

We do not obtain information about you from data brokers, marketing partners, or social media platforms, and we do not use any information for targeted advertising.

2. How we process your information

In short: To answer calls, book appointments, notify our clients, keep the service running, and comply with the law.
  • To answer calls and schedule appointments on behalf of our business clients. We use information collected during a call to answer inbound telephone calls placed to our clients' phone numbers, gather the details of your service request, schedule appointments in the client's calendar, and notify the client of the call and its outcome.
  • To identify and escalate emergency service requests. We analyze the content of a call to determine whether you are reporting an urgent or emergency situation, and if so, immediately notify our business client by phone and text message so they can respond.
  • To deliver and facilitate delivery of services.
  • To send administrative information, including appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • To respond to inquiries and offer support.
  • To fulfill and manage orders and billing for our business clients.
  • To facilitate account creation and authentication where an account is offered.
  • To protect our services, including fraud monitoring and prevention.
  • To evaluate and improve our services and identify usage trends.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not process your information to send marketing or promotional communications, or for targeted advertising.

3. When and with whom we share your personal information

In short: With the business you called, and with the service providers that make the service work.

We share your data with third-party vendors and service providers who perform services for us and require access to that information to do their work. We have contracts in place with each of them designed to safeguard your personal information. They cannot use your information for their own purposes.

The third parties we share personal information with are:

  • VAPI — voice AI platform. Receives call audio and caller information to operate the automated assistant.
  • Twilio — telephony and SMS delivery. Receives caller phone numbers and message content.
  • OpenAI — language processing. Receives call transcript content to generate responses.
  • Deepgram — speech recognition. Receives call audio to produce transcripts.
  • Google — cloud storage and calendar. Receives caller information and appointment details for scheduling and record keeping.
  • Stripe — payment processing for business client billing.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Our business clients — receive information about callers who telephoned their business, including name, contact details, and service request.

Business transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.

4. Artificial intelligence

In short: Our service is powered by AI. You can ask for a human at any time.

We offer products and features powered by artificial intelligence and machine learning. We provide these through third-party AI service providers, including VAPI, Deepgram, and OpenAI. Your input, output, and personal information are shared with and processed by these providers to enable the service.

Our AI products are designed for AI automation, natural language processing, automated speech recognition and speech synthesis, and answering inbound telephone calls.

How to opt out

Callers who prefer not to speak with an automated assistant may say so at any point during the call. The assistant will take a message and notify the business owner to call you back directly.

You may also request that we delete any recording or transcript of your call by contacting us at cbosman@praxisprotocol.io. Where an account is offered, you can update your preferences in your account settings.

6. How we keep your information safe

In short: We use organizational and technical security measures, but no system is perfectly secure.

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the personal information we process. These include encrypted credential storage, two-factor authentication on all platform accounts, encrypted transmission, access restrictions on administrative systems, and defined retention limits with scheduled deletion.

Despite these safeguards, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise that unauthorized third parties will never defeat our security and improperly access your information.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature you can activate to signal a preference not to have your online browsing activity monitored. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals. If a standard is adopted that we must follow, we will describe that practice in a revised version of this policy.

Our website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind.

11. Automated call answering and recording

When you telephone a business that uses Halfbeat AI, your call is answered by an automated assistant rather than a person. At the beginning of every call, we tell you that you are speaking with an automated assistant and that the call is being recorded. By continuing the call after that notice, you consent to the recording.

We apply this practice to every call regardless of the state you are calling from, including states that require all parties to consent to recording.

If you would prefer not to speak with an automated assistant, you may say so at any point during the call. The assistant will take a message and notify the business owner to call you back directly.

The recording and transcript of your call are used to schedule your appointment, notify the business you called, improve the accuracy of the assistant, and resolve any dispute about what was discussed. Recordings and transcripts are deleted after 90 days.

You may request deletion of the recording and transcript of your call at any time by contacting us at cbosman@praxisprotocol.io.
